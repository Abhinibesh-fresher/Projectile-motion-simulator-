How it works

The ball is launched from height h at angle θ with speed v. Gravity g pulls it straight down and there is no air resistance, so the horizontal and vertical motions are independent.

Horizontal position x(t) = v · cos θ · t
Vertical position y(t) = h + v · sin θ · t − ½ · g · t²
Velocity components vx = v · cos θ  ·  vy(t) = v · sin θ − g · t
Time of flight t = ( v·sin θ + √((v·sin θ)² + 2gh) ) / g
Maximum height H = h + (v · sin θ)² / (2g)
Range R = v · cos θ · tflight

Maximum height is reached at t = v·sin θ / g. If the ball is launched straight up (θ = 90°) it has no horizontal velocity, so the range is zero. Long flights are played back slightly faster; the times shown are always true simulation seconds.
